# How to Enable App Passwords for Gmail

## The Issue
You're seeing "App passwords is not available" because **2-Step Verification must be enabled first**.

## Solution: Enable 2-Step Verification

### Step 1: Enable 2-Step Verification

1. Go to: **https://myaccount.google.com/security**
2. Sign in with **vatsal.mishra7794@gmail.com**
3. Scroll down to **"Signing in to Google"** section
4. Find **"2-Step Verification"**
5. Click on it
6. Click **"Get started"** or **"Turn on"**
7. Follow the setup process:
   - Enter your password
   - Add your phone number
   - Verify with a code sent to your phone
   - Confirm the setup

### Step 2: Wait a Few Minutes

After enabling 2-Step Verification, wait 2-3 minutes for Google to update your account settings.

### Step 3: Try App Passwords Again

1. Go to: **https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords**
2. You should now see the App Passwords page
3. If you still don't see it, try:
   - Sign out and sign back in
   - Wait a few more minutes
   - Clear browser cache

### Step 4: Generate App Password

Once you can access App Passwords:

1. Select app: **"Mail"**
2. Select device: **"Other (Custom name)"**
3. Type: **"Wedding RSVP"**
4. Click **"Generate"**
5. Copy the 16-character password (remove spaces!)

### Alternative: If App Passwords Still Don't Work

If you still can't access App Passwords after enabling 2-Step Verification, it might be because:
- Your account is a Google Workspace account (managed by organization)
- Your account has restrictions

**In that case, we can use an alternative email service like:**
- Resend (easier setup)
- SendGrid
- Mailgun
